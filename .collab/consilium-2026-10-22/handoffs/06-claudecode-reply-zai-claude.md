## Framing — challenge first

The premise 'the finder would confirm its own frame' is only half supported: pass 2's findings are *missed error paths and races*, not design disputes — the finder's one-line remedies were directions and the worker's designs were reasonable but incomplete. Enumeration, not taste, is failing. To choose between these I would need the pass-to-closure history over more than the two passes observed, and the cost of one wrongly adopted design.

## Q1 — roles and verifier

Sound, with the verifier split in two: (1) a test the solver must ship — failing before, green after — which the coordinator RUNS (mechanical, no model); (2) the finder re-checks only its own trigger replay, binary closed/not-closed, and never sees rival designs, which removes the 'finder rejects every foreign fix' mode. A third reviewer adds cost and no authority. Solver-not-knowing-the-codebase is a pack problem: carry the named functions, their callers, and existing tests, not the tree.
