## Framing challenge

The brief assumes the missing step is a better remedy. The evidence points elsewhere: pass 1's eight findings were all "fixed", and pass 2 found the *remaining cases*. The defect is verification of the fix, not imagination of it. Options the brief does not list:

- Finder ships acceptance criteria + invariant + test skeleton with the finding (no new consultation).
- One solver, not a panel of three; or the worker proposes-then-codes in the same wave.
- Remedy panel triggered only on a *reopened* finding (F91-1/F91-2 qualify), not every blocker.
- Do nothing: spend the same window on a longer finder verification pass.

To choose I would need: the re-failure rate per finding class, solver proposal adoption rate in a one-release pilot, pack-only vs repo-reading solver quality, and each lab's real window scarcity. Without those, build the pilot, not the machinery.

## Q1. Roles and verifier

Separation is sound; vendor exclusion is the weak part (vendor is a poor proxy for independence, and may exclude the best solver — exclude the finder *instance* instead). Failure modes: pack-only solvers hallucinate code that is not there; finders reject foreign fixes that threaten their frame; three consultations per blocker at ~280 s and 9–16 % of a window each does not scale past a few blockers.

Verifier: dual-key. The solver's shipped test is primary evidence, the finder confirms its original trigger is closed against criteria it wrote *before* seeing proposals, and a third member adjudicates only on dispute or second failure. Test-only verification fails — this codebase's history is implementer tests encoding the implementer's frame.

## Q2. Minimal design

`proposals[]` per finding: `{finding_id, proposer{provider, model, consult_id}, base_commit, approach, locations[], invariant, patch_sketch, test{name, drives, asserts}, risks, confidence}`, stored verbatim in `findings.json`; coordinator adds `{status: adopted|rejected, reason}`. Brief = finding record + pack of located code and existing tests. Existing statuses (`proposed|implemented|verified`) carry the lifecycle.

Must NOT be automated: adoption, merging proposals, implementation, verification/closure, and the decision to spend the window. The `--remedy` offer on HOLD is the right level of automation.

## Q3. Live remedies

**F91-1 (blocker)** — Move attempt derivation under the lock: `claim_for_spawn` ([mod.rs](/crates/c3/src/work/mod.rs:747)) takes an expectation (resumable statuses, cap), rereads under `admission_lock`, refuses terminal/exhausted/foreign-live records, derives `attempt = locked quota.resumes + 1`, and writes `running` in the same locked write; `resume_once` ([mod.rs](/crates/c3/src/work/mod.rs:1137)) stops precomputing `n`. Invariant: no spawn unless the locked record was resumable at claim instant. Tests: `claim_for_spawn_rejects_terminal_and_exhausted`, `claim_for_spawn_derives_attempt_from_locked_record`.

**F91-2 (blocker)** — Fail closed on uninspectable records: `read_record` ([record.rs](/crates/c3/src/work/record.rs:137)) maps only `NotFound` to `Ok(None)`; `run_dirs`/`live_run`/`other_live_run` return `Result` and both admission paths refuse on error. Invariant: admission only over inspected-or-confirmed-absent records. Tests: `read_record_distinguishes_notfound_from_unreadable`, `admission_refuses_when_candidate_uninspectable`.

**F91-4 (major)** — Require affirmative terminal evidence: Claude success only from `subtype=="success"` with explicit `is_error:false` (new `has_success_result` in [record.rs](/crates/c3/src/work/record.rs:544)); Codex success only from `turn.completed`, deleting the `agent_message` arm in `terminal_outcome` ([engines.rs](/crates/c3/src/work/engines.rs:481)) while keeping `agent_message` for report extraction. Update `terminal_outcome_reads_the_stream` to expect `None` for both incomplete streams.

## Q4. Scoring

Split, delayed, denominated. Finder credit at verified closure of a real finding; solver credit only when an adopted proposal is implemented *and verified* without material redesign (`partly` if reworked). Adoption alone scores nothing. Weight by severity and recurrence; count rejected/duplicate findings and rejected proposals in the denominator so volume farming dilutes itself. Keep finder/solver/verifier as three router dimensions with a minimum sample before solver routing bites.
