## Framing (before the numbered answers)

The brief frames the loop failure as *remedy quality*. The pass-1→pass-2 record frames it as *verification*: "the fix closed the simple case, this is the remaining case" — nobody drove the finder's trigger. Options the brief omits:

1. **Closing-test obligation** — nothing moves to `implemented` without a regression test reproducing the finder's stated trigger. Addresses the observed failure mode at a fraction of panel cost.
2. **Trigger-first solvers** — a proposal that does not cover the trigger is inadmissible on its face.
3. **Repeat-offender gate** — fund the panel only where a finding `supersedes` an earlier one (exactly F91-1/3/4); first-time findings keep the cheap one-line-remedy path.
4. **Verifier swap** — rotate the standing acceptance reviewer instead of seating a third consultation per blocker.

To choose among these I need: how many reopens are coverage gaps vs design gaps; whether solver models can read the repository (pack-only solvers cannot check call sites); Astra's rate of rejecting fixes it did not design; and one panel seat vs one worker wave in cost.
