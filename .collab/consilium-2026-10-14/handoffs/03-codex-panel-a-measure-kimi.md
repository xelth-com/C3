## Framing challenge first

The brief bundles one safe decision with two unsafe ones. Extending the closed vocabulary is nearly free: `classes.rs` already maps anything unknown to `other`/`unknown`, so new values never break old intakes. The risky parts are (a) committing to a *published score* before the denominators are comparable, and (b) assuming the hook can see effort.

Options the brief does not list:

- **(5) Tier-split panels as the experiment.** Alternative 2 (local A/B) is dismissed as "nothing learned for other users" — but the routed panel already exists. Send the same brief at two efforts to two seats of one panel; the existing consult events capture it for everyone once `effort` ships. This is alternative 1 and 2 combined, at panel cost.
- **(6) Publish counts, keep scores local.** `c3 scoreboard` is already local. Ship only `effort` on events and let each install compute its own usefulness-per-cost; the intake publishes distributions, not rankings. Defers the irreversible "published metric" decision until the findings join (alternative 3) exists.
- **(7) Record effort now, commit to no score at all.** Schema first, semantics later.

What I would need to choose: does z.ai expose more than three glm-5.3 tiers; does `SubagentStop` stdin (or the transcript metadata) carry the Agent call's effort override; how is the intake schema versioned today.

## Q1 — tiers and denominator

Tiers: gpt-6-astra (window-bound): chore low, checkpoint/reading medium, diff-review/acceptance high, core-contract/stuck xhigh. glm-5.3 (money-bound, no 5-hour window): chore low, everything else high, stuck max — the asymmetry is real and the policy should encode it. Denominator: findings-confirmed per unit of the *binding constraint* — window-share for OpenAI, money for GLM, wall time only as a guard. Finding F1: window share is not a cross-vendor denominator (quota.rs reads Codex rollouts only), so "usefulness per window share" cannot be the single published score. Design not listed: score the **marginal finding** — findings no other panel seat produced — per cost unit; it survives routed panels where seats differ.

## Q2 — worker capture

`SubagentStop` is the right capture point for Claude Code; other hosts report via the coordinator's own `rate` path with tier `other`. Must stay out: transcript/cwd/repo paths, session id (hash it), the agent label as typed. Effort: do not trust the transcript — the coordinator knows effort at spawn; have it write a local worker-id→{tier,effort} registry the hook reads (zero tokens, no parsing). Attribution under rework: add outcome `reworked`, marked at rating time; credit completion to the last worker, charge the rework against the original tier's score. Without this the original tier's completion share is inflated (F5).

## Q3 — add to the vocabulary now

`effort` (sent, closed none|low|medium|high|xhigh|max), `effort_requested` (preset name or `other`), `effort_native` (pre-mapping label — otherwise medium→high is indistinguishable from native high later, F2), `cost_basis` (window|money|none) so denominators stay interpretable, and `schema_v` per event. Worker event: tier, effort, outcome class, duration/token buckets, verified flag, resume count, reworked flag.

## Q4 — scout and judge

Failure modes: the scout summarizes the anomaly away; the judge anchors on the scout's conclusion; a cold-cache digest silently drops context. Rules: the scout emits a **fixed schema**, never a verdict — file list, hunk ids, suspicion 0–3 per hunk — capped at ~200 lines; the judge always gets the raw file list and may fetch the raw diff of any hunk (a tool call, not a bigger digest). If the judge cannot fetch raw context, the routing saves money by silently lowering review quality — that is the real cost cap to set.
